<h2 id="about-me">About Me</h2>

<p>I'm an M.S. student at the <a href="https://ct.kaist.ac.kr/">Graduate School of Culture Technology, KAIST</a>, and a member of the <a href="https://vml.kaist.ac.kr/">Visual Media Lab</a>, advised by <a href="https://vml.kaist.ac.kr/main/people/person/1">Prof. Junyong Noh</a>.</p>

<p>My research aims to bridge the gap between 2D visual knowledge and 3D graphics for digital avatars. I am interested in transferring the rich priors learned by generative models to 3D representations to support flexible avatar creation and editing. My recent work explores head avatar stylization and facial identity across visual styles.</p>

<p>I received my B.S. in Convergence Engineering from the School of Undergraduate Studies, College of Transdisciplinary Studies, at <a href="https://www.dgist.ac.kr/eng/">DGIST</a> in February 2025.</p>

<h2 id="research-interests">Research Interests</h2>

<ul>
  <li><strong>2D-to-3D Knowledge Transfer:</strong> bringing visual knowledge from 2D generative models into 3D representations.</li>
  <li><strong>Digital Avatars:</strong> generative approaches to controllable avatar creation, stylization, and editing.</li>
</ul>
